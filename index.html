1. Printer
  1. Switch the printer on and wait until the light stops blinking.
  2. Click Connect Bluetooth or Connect USB. The page reads the label roll that is loaded. The printer stays connected until you switch it off or close this page.
Printer not connected
Which one do I pick in the window?

Bluetooth: pick the entry that starts with B1- and click Pair. If the list stays empty, the printer is off, or it is still connected to the NIIMBOT app on a phone (close the app). The page asks each time you connect over Bluetooth.

USB: plug the cable in first. The printer usually shows as B1, usbmodem… (Mac) or USB Serial Device (COM…) (Windows). If you are not sure, unplug the cable while the window is open, then plug it back in: the entry that disappears and comes back is the printer. You only pick it once per computer; after that, Connect USB connects without asking.
