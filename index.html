3. Printer
  1. Load the label and check the preview above.
  2. Switch the printer on and wait until the light stops blinking.
  3. Click Connect Bluetooth or Connect USB. The printer stays connected until you switch it off or close this page, so you can print several labels without connecting again.
  4. Click Print. It starts right away.
Printer not connected
Which one do I pick in the window?

Bluetooth: pick the entry that starts with B1- and click Pair. If the list stays empty, the printer is off, or it is still connected to the NIIMBOT app on a phone (close the app). The page asks each time you connect over Bluetooth.

USB: plug the cable in first. The printer usually shows as B1, usbmodem… (Mac) or USB Serial Device (COM…) (Windows). If you are not sure, unplug the cable while the window is open, then plug it back in: the entry that disappears and comes back is the printer. You only pick it once per computer; after that, Connect USB connects without asking.

Print direction is fixed to top, so each print fits exactly on one label.

Print tuning (saved per computer; left margin separately for each label type)   (automatic)   (lower = thinner)  

Move the label with the left margin. The right margin follows on its own, so the print keeps the full width of the chosen label type.
